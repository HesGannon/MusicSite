Top Songs

From Chart-Toppers to Fan Favorites

These are Kanye West's biggest songs, from the chart-topping singles to the streaming giants. Underneath them you'll find the five songs loaded into this site's music player, which you can play right from this page.

The Top 10

  1. 808s & Heartbreak · 2008

    Heartless

    In 2026 this became his first song to pass 2 billion streams on Spotify.

  2. Graduation · 2007

    Stronger

    Built on a Daft Punk sample, this No. 1 hit is still one of his most-streamed songs ever.

  3. Late Registration · 2005

    Gold Digger (feat. Jamie Foxx)

    His first No. 1 as a lead artist. It spent 10 weeks at the top of the Billboard Hot 100.

  4. Graduation · 2007

    Flashing Lights (feat. Dwele)

    Smooth, glossy and still one of his most-played songs, nearly 20 years later.

  5. My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy · 2010

    Runaway (feat. Pusha T)

    A nine-minute epic that starts with a single piano note. He also made a short film around it.

  6. Graduation · 2007

    I Wonder

    A fan favorite built around a sample of Labi Siffre's "My Song."

  7. My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy · 2010

    Power

    Huge drums and chanting vocals. This was the lead single that kicked off the Fantasy era.

  8. The Life of Pablo · 2016

    Father Stretch My Hands Pt. 1

    A short, euphoric gospel-meets-trap track that became one of his most-played songs of the 2010s.

  9. Graduation · 2007

    Can't Tell Me Nothing

    The first single from Graduation and his anthem about fame and doubt.

  10. Vultures 1 · 2024

    Carnival (with Ty Dolla $ign)

    His first No. 1 in 13 years. It made him the first rapper to top the Hot 100 in three different decades.
